01 — About

Code is how I think. Design is how I communicate it.

I'm a Software Engineering & Information Systems student who got into programming through curiosity and stayed for the problem-solving. Somewhere along the way, design became just as central to how I work — I care as much about how something looks and feels as I do about how it runs.

My interests span full-stack web development, UI/UX and graphic design, applied AI, and the fundamentals of cybersecurity. I like projects that force me to learn something new, and I like finishing things — a working product beats a perfect idea that never shipped.
